## 2. What Inkey is, and what it isn't

Inkey is a tool for tenants and landlords to record, month by month, the payments of a lease and confirm them between the two of them. With those records, the tenant can build a history and share it whenever they want.

So there's no misunderstanding, Inkey **is not**:

- **A rental agency or an intermediary.** We don't look for properties or tenants, we don't negotiate leases, and we're not a party to any lease.
- **A payment method.** We don't receive, hold or transfer money. Payments happen outside Inkey, however the parties agree.
- **A credit report.** We don't check banks, credit bureaus or debtor databases, and the history is not a risk score.
- **Legal, accounting or financial advice.** Nothing Inkey shows replaces a professional's advice.

## 5. How the history works

- Each month, the tenant reports a payment and the landlord confirms it or says it hasn't arrived yet.
- Only what **both sides confirm** counts towards the history. An unconfirmed month doesn't add up, and it isn't shown as anything negative either.
- Inkey records what the parties declare and confirm. **We don't verify** on our own that payments were made, that amounts are correct, or that whoever presents themselves as the landlord actually is one. Trust in the history depends on both sides telling the truth.
- A confirmed lease can't be edited unilaterally, so nobody can change the other side's history.
