# Inkey Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 21 September 2026

> **This is a courtesy translation.** Inkey operates in Argentina and the binding version of this policy is the Spanish one. If the two differ, [the Spanish version](/privacidad) prevails.

At Inkey we handle information that matters to you: where you live, how much you pay, and your history with a lease. This policy explains what data we keep, what for, who we share it with, and how you can control it. It's governed by **Argentina's Personal Data Protection Act (Ley 25.326)** and its supplementary rules.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The party responsible for your data is Inkey, a service developed in the Argentine Republic.

For any question about your data: **contacto@inkeyapp.com**.

## 2. What data we keep

**What you give us:**

- **Account:** first name, last name, email and mobile number.
- **Lease:** address, neighbourhood, lease dates, amount, currency, day rent is due, and adjustment index. (In Argentina rent is typically adjusted periodically against an official index, such as the ICL published by the central bank.)
- **Payments:** amount, payment date, confirmation status and, if you upload them, proof of payment.
- **Notes between the parties:** if the landlord marks a payment as not yet received, they can leave a note explaining why. It's stored and seen only by the two sides of that lease: it never reaches the profile.
- **Documents:** the lease, if you choose to upload it.
- **Reviews:** the tags and the text you write when a lease ends.
- **Waiting list:** your email and whether you're a tenant or a landlord, if you signed up before launch.

**What's generated by using Inkey:**

- The profile links you create and how many times they were opened.
- Technical and security records, such as the date of important actions on your account. To prevent abuse we use an encrypted, irreversible version of your IP address: we don't store the IP itself.

**What we don't keep or check:** we don't ask for or check credit data, we don't access your bank accounts, we don't consult debtor databases, and we don't ask for sensitive data such as health, religion or political opinions.

## 3. What we use it for

We use your data only to:

- Provide the service: record your lease, the payments and the confirmations, and build your history.
- Send you the emails needed to use Inkey: access to your account, invitations, notices of payments to confirm, and receipts.
- Show your profile to the people you decide to share it with.
- Protect the security of the service and prevent fraud and abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations.

**We don't sell or rent your data**, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **With the other side of your lease.** The landlord and the tenant see the data of the lease they share, the payments, the proof of payment and the lease document. They don't see your other leases or your activity outside that lease.
- **With whoever receives your profile link.** They only see what the profile shows: your first name and the initial of your last name, the neighbourhoods, your payment metrics and published reviews. They never see your full address, your email, your phone number or your proof of payment. Amounts are shown only if you turn that on for that link.
- **With providers that make Inkey work**, who process the data only to provide us their service:
  - **Supabase:** database, files and account access. Servers in the United States (Oregon).
  - **Vercel:** site hosting. Servers in the United States and other countries.
  - **Resend:** email delivery. Servers in the United States.
  - **Google Fonts:** the site's typefaces.
- **With authorities**, only where a law or a court order requires it.

## 5. Data outside Argentina

Our providers are abroad, so your data is stored and processed outside Argentina, mainly in the **United States**.

Ley 25.326 prohibits transferring personal data to countries without an adequate level of protection, unless certain exceptions apply (article 12). The United States is not among the countries Argentina considers adequate, so that transfer rests on two things:

- **Your consent**, which you give by using Inkey knowing that our providers are abroad, as this policy explains.
- **The contractual safeguards we have with each provider.** With Supabase, Vercel and Resend their data processing agreements apply, which require them to process your data only on our instructions, to maintain appropriate security measures, not to use it for their own purposes, and to comply with standard contractual clauses for international transfers.

If we ever change provider or region, we'll reflect it in this policy before doing so.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **While your account is active**, we keep your data to provide the service.
- **If you close your account:**
  - We delete your personal data (first name, last name, mobile) and your email is freed up.
  - Your profile links stop working immediately.
  - Reviews you received are hidden, and the ones you wrote become anonymous.
  - Proof of payment and leases you uploaded are deleted after 30 days. During that period, the other side of the lease can download them, because they're part of their record too, and we let them know by email.
  - The confirmed payment history is kept **only for the other side**, who keeps seeing their own history. There you appear as "Closed account", without your personal data.
- Technical and security records are kept for as long as needed to protect the service and comply with legal obligations.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to **access** your data, **rectify** it, **update** it and **request its deletion**, free of charge, under articles 14, 15 and 16 of Ley 25.326. Access can be exercised free of charge every six months, unless you demonstrate a legitimate interest in doing so sooner.

You can exercise them in two ways:

- **From Inkey**, in **Your account**: edit your details, download a full copy of your information, or close your account.
- **By email** to **contacto@inkeyapp.com**. We answer access requests within 10 calendar days, and rectification or deletion requests within 5 business days.

**The AGENCIA DE ACCESO A LA INFORMACIÓN PÚBLICA, as the supervisory authority under Ley N° 25.326, has the power to hear complaints and claims brought by those whose rights are affected by non-compliance with personal data protection rules in force.**

## 8. How we protect your data

- All communication with Inkey travels encrypted.
- Each user can only access the data of the leases they take part in: that rule is enforced in the database itself, not just in the application.
- Proof of payment and leases are kept in private storage and opened with temporary links that expire in a minute.
- Invitation and profile links are stored as an irreversible fingerprint: the link itself isn't stored anywhere.

No system is infallible. If an incident affecting your data occurred, we'll inform you without delay.

## 9. Minors

Inkey is only for people over 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors. If we detect a minor's account, we close it.

## 10. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and protect your account. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If we add any measurement tool in the future, we'll say so in this policy before turning it on.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we'll tell you by email or inside Inkey before it takes effect.

## 12. Contact

For any question or request about your data: **contacto@inkeyapp.com**.
